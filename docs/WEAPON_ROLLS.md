# Officer weapon bonus rolls — v0.3.3

Development source adds a validated owned-copy bonus-layout transfer and
filtered owned-copy element action. See the
[current game-specific expansion](GAME_SPECIFIC_EXPANSION.md#dw3-complete-edition-remastered)
for destination restrictions and preserved acquisition data.

The Weapons tab now edits normal bonus types and values on owned regular,
fourth and fifth weapon copies. It writes a supported fusion result directly,
without consuming another weapon or changing achievement/story records.
Open a separate copy and keep its automatic backup.

## Controls

Select an owned copy. Choose normal bonus types and their verified values in
the nine saved slots, then Apply Weapon Bonuses. Rare bonus types and elements
also have verified controls; see [WEAPON_ATTRIBUTES_FIX.md](WEAPON_ATTRIBUTES_FIX.md).
Max Selected Existing Bonus Rolls
raises the current normal bonuses without adding or replacing their types.
Existing higher saved rolls remain unchanged; a Max action never lowers them.
Max All Owned Bonus Rolls applies this to all supported owned copies,
including unique weapons you have just unlocked in the editor. View-only
copies are counted and preserved. Save As writes a new file with a byte report.

Base Attack is the weapon definition's fixed Power; it is separate from the
Attack bonus supplied by Tiger Amulet. There is no per-copy saved Power field.
Hit flags, WeaponID/ID, DataID, GetTime and equipped references are preserved.
Elements and rare skills change only through explicit controls. The editor
does not simulate material selection, deletion, unique resets or achievements.

|---|---|---:|
| Speed Scroll | Speed | 24 |
| Wing Boots | Jump | 24 |
| Peacock Urn | Life | 90 |
| Dragon Amulet | Musou | 90 |
| Tiger Amulet | Attack | 30 |
| Tortoise Amulet | Defense | 60 |
| Huang's Bow | Bow Attack | 60 |
| Shell Armor | Bow Defense | 60 |
| Horned Helm | Mounted Attack | 60 |
| Cavalry Armor | Mounted Defense | 60 |
| Seven Star Orb | Luck | 30 |
| Wind Scroll | Reach | 30 |
| Elixir | Musou Charge | 30 |
| Mountain Quiver | Starting Arrows | 48 |
| Mountain Pouch | Meat Bun Recovery | 30 |
| Bronze Flask | Charge | 18 |

These are weapon-material rolls, not the ordinary equipped-item caps.
The drop generator uses four tiers and can apply a 150% correction with
specific float32 rounding. This creates gaps: Speed +19/+22 and Attack
+22/+25/+28 cannot be generated by this verified branch. Dropdowns use
exact reachable sets from weapon_bonus_rules.json, rather than all integers
below the maximum. Unique stock values outside those sets are allowed only
on the corresponding definition. Volcano Staff's stock Attack +43 is one
example; it is not a donor maximum and cannot be granted to ordinary weapons.

| Weapon rank | Maximum normal bonuses |
|---|---:|
| 1st, 2nd, 3rd | 6 |
| 4th | 7 |
| 5th | 8 |

Nine serialized slots include normal and rare bonuses together. Duplicate
identities are refused. At most one rare bonus is supported. Rare bonuses can
appear before the final slot: the
supplied fused regular copy36 has six normal bonuses followed by Hex Mark
Saddle at slot6 (zero-based). Numeric-only edits preserve that rare position.
Rare additions/replacements use the native final slot, retaining normal order.

For conservative reachability, uniques retain at least their stock normal
bonus count; non-starter regular weapons retain at least one. Existing types
can be replaced with other eligible normal bonuses. Empty slots can be filled
within the rank limit. Initial starter weapons may have zero normal bonuses.
Unknown identities, values or references are view-only rather than rewritten.
The Remove The Grind preset still acquires stock uniques and preserves their
existing properties. Weapon roll maximization is an explicit separate action.

## Native evidence and storage

WeaponDataArray contains500 tagged WeaponSaveData records; UniqueWeaponDataArray
contains84. Skill is nine tagged EquipItemSaveData records, each containing
an ordinary enum ID, bodyguard enum sentinel and Int32 Value. Disk boundaries
are found by parsing UE tags; runtime strides are not disk offsets.

Native ordinary pool0x1515710..0x1515759 includes all16shipped Type0normal IDs
(0-12,25-27). Generator call0x15157F0 uses weaponflag1. The base formula at
0x1536436..0x15364F1 is tier*ValueRand+random1..ValueRand; correction
0x15364F8..0x1536569 uses the shipped probability/ratio and exact SSE operations.
Metadata records the stage tables, rounding constants and reachable donors.

Fusion0x169894A..0x1698A61 copies selected material ID/value into a selected
normal destination slot. There is no addition or further amplification.
Rank getter0x15358D0 uses WeaponID, not the Attr flags; fusion commit
0x1697F1D reads the shipped MaxBlueSkillNum row. Setter0x1516300 writes only
the chosen inventory record and does not update officer equipment references
or the acquisition collection cache. The full game separately consumes source
weapons via0x1516270 and sets RecordSaveData.bPathOfAMasterSmith; these are
not required for a direct bonus edit and remain untouched.

Regular DataID equals inventory slot0-499; unique DataID equals10000+unique
slot. Combined acquisition and bonus editing constructs the final unique
inventory skills once, while creating a missing first-acquisition cache with
stock values. Existing collection caches remain unchanged, matching fusion.

Confidence is high for supported numeric transfer outcomes, rank counts,
stock exceptions and preserved dependencies. Minimum-count floors are
conservative editor restrictions. In-game acceptance,
material-consuming workflows and different versions remain untested.
